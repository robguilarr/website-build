Hi, I’m Roberto a person on a lifelong pursuit to become the best version of myself through Kaizen principles, to grow into someone I’ll always be proud of.

I’m an AI Engineer driven by simple yet useful principles: continuous improvement, people-first collaboration, and impact measured through customer value. That’s the thread connecting my work today — building reliable AI systems that turn ambiguous problems into tangible business growth.

Since I was young, I’ve always loved learning on my own. At 15, I taught myself how to program video games with Unity, following MonoBehaviour patterns. Interestingly, modern agentic frameworks use similar state and memory management principles as NPCs, which made my transition into AI surprisingly natural.

I grew up in a small town called Ciudad Colón, in Costa Rica, but my career has taken me across the world to work on major initiatives alongside incredibly skilled and educated people I never expected to meet — and sometimes to compete (and even win) against them in hackathons.

Last but not least — I usually practice trail running at dawn, keep learning about neural nets (transformers, etc.), watch la Vecchia Signora games, my favorite Pokémon is Snorlax, and sometimes unwind by playing on my Switch.
And a fun fact: I live with a golden holland lop bunny named Jacka, born in January of the Chinese New Year 2023.
